# 🛡️ Alehulsh (አለሁልሽ)
### Ethiopia's Lifeline Emergency Safety App for Women

**Alehulsh** is a women-focused emergency safety application designed to help women in Ethiopia quickly request help, share their location, and alert nearby people and trusted emergency contacts when they are in danger.

Our mission is to make women feel safer by connecting them to people who can help when they need it most.

---

## 🌍 About the Project

Alehulsh (አለሁልሽ) is a social-impact technology initiative addressing women's safety and emergency response challenges in Ethiopia.

In emergency situations, every second matters. However, accessing immediate assistance is not always easy. Alehulsh aims to reduce the time between asking for help and receiving it by creating a community-connected emergency response system.

With a simple SOS mechanism, users can send distress alerts containing their location and emergency information to nearby Alehulsh users and their trusted emergency contacts.

**We believe that no woman should have to face an emergency alone.**

## ✨ Key Features

- **SOS Emergency Activation:** Quickly trigger an emergency alert through the in-app SOS button or by pressing the phone's power button five times.
- **Real-Time Location Sharing:** Share your location through Google Maps so responders can identify where help is needed.
- **Nearby Community Alerts:** Notify Alehulsh users within a 500-meter radius of an emergency.
- **Emergency Contacts:** Send alerts to registered trusted contacts regardless of their distance.
- **Voice & Text Messages:** Include a voice recording or text message to provide additional context about the emergency.
- **Emergency Alerts Dashboard:** View and manage incoming emergency alerts.
- **Community-Based Response:** Connect women with nearby people who may be able to provide immediate assistance.

## 🏗️ How It Works

1. **Emergency Activation:** A user activates SOS through the application or the supported power-button shortcut.
2. **Alert Creation:** The system creates an emergency alert containing the user's location and available emergency information.
3. **Location-Based Distribution:** Nearby Alehulsh users within a 500-meter radius receive an emergency notification.
4. **Trusted Contact Notification:** The user's registered emergency contacts are notified regardless of their location.
5. **Response:** Recipients can view the emergency details and location to help the person in need.
